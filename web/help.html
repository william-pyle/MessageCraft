MessageSolutions

How to pair MessageSolutions

Run the installer (MessageSolutions-windows-x64.msi). It installs MessageSolutions into Program Files, adds it to the Start menu and can open it when it finishes. Prefer no installer? Use the portable zip: unzip it and open MessageSolutions.exe, keeping the helper program beside it.

Pair with your phone

  1. In MessageSolutions, click Sign in with Google.
  2. Sign in with the same Google account your phone's Google Messages uses.
  3. Google Messages on your phone shows a few emoji: tap the one MessageSolutions shows.

Older phones whose Device pairing screen has a QR code scanner can choose Use a QR code instead and scan the code.

You only pair once. Your phone needs to stay on and online for MessageSolutions to sync and send.

Unpairing

In MessageSolutions open File › Info and choose Unpair this computer. That also deletes the copy of your conversations kept on the computer.

Privacy

MessageSolutions talks only to Google Messages, the same service your phone and Google Messages for web use. Your pairing, settings and a copy of recent conversations stay on your computer, in your Windows account's own folder; the pairing is encrypted for your account.

Send feedback

Something not working? Note what you saw and when, and include the helper's log from the MessageSolutions data folder (File › Info › Open data folder).
