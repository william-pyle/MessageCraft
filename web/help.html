MessageCraft

How to pair MessageCraft

Run the installer (MessageCraft-windows-x64.msi). It installs MessageCraft into Program Files, adds it to the Start menu and can open it when it finishes. Prefer no installer? Use the portable zip: unzip it and open MessageCraft.exe, keeping the helper program beside it.

Pair with your phone

  1. MessageCraft shows a QR code when it first opens.
  2. On your Android phone, open Google Messages and tap your profile picture (or the ⋮ menu).
  3. Tap Device pairing, then QR code scanner, and point your phone at the code.

You only pair once. Your phone needs to stay on and online for MessageCraft to sync and send.

Unpairing

In MessageCraft open File › Info and choose Unpair this computer. That also deletes the copy of your conversations kept on the computer.

Privacy

MessageCraft talks only to Google Messages, the same service your phone and Google Messages for web use. Your pairing, settings and a copy of recent conversations stay on your computer, in your Windows account's own folder; the pairing is encrypted for your account.

Send feedback

Something not working? Note what you saw and when, and include the helper's log from the MessageCraft data folder (File › Info › Open data folder).
